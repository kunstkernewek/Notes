# Kunst Kerwhatnow? 

Kunst Kernewek lounged into existence around the turn of the millennium. 
It was not-very-organised to support Artists, Curation, Exhibits and Events that developed out of and after the initial [Koept](http://koe.pt) level events.

`Kunst` (meaning "art", originally "knowledge, skill") traces back through the Germanic languages to a Proto-Indo-European root meaning "to know". 
In this context it is simply an international term to signify the type of stuff being represented - Works of art.

`Kernewek` is derived from the Cornish word Kernow (Cornwall), combined with the adjectival/language suffix -ek ("pertaining to" or "language of"). 
In this context it is used to denote that the stuff being represented is of Cornish heritage.

This label has been applied to various projects over the years, often in a promotional sense.
These projects have been largely funded by [an Electronic Arts organisation](http://Electronic-Arts.org).

Kunst Kernewek is of Koept origin - promoting Cornish electronic arts very lightly in the early second millennium.
